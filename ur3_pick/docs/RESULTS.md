## Part B - sensing

Intrinsics from 40 checkerboard views (7x6 inner corners, 25 mm squares), pinhole with square pixels and the principal point fixed at the image centre:

* `fx = fy = 171.78 px`, `cx = 159.5`, `cy = 119.5`
* reprojection RMS **0.194 px**; focal-length error vs the simulator's true value **+0.40 px**
* hand-eye `T_tool_cam` by Park's method: translation error **1.03 mm**, rotation error **0.16 deg**

Detection noise, split into a white part (varies between frames at a fixed pose) and a bias part (varies with viewpoint, so averaging cannot remove it). Variances are fitted as `a + b r^2` against camera-object range `r`:

| quantity | white `a` | white `b` | bias RMS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| x, y | 6.70e-09 m^2 | 1.46e-07 | 0.50 mm |
| z | 0.00e+00 m^2 | 4.14e-07 | 0.89 mm |
| yaw | 4.36e-05 rad^2 | 7.05e-04 | 0.45 deg |

The bias term is added to the object KF's reported covariance; without it the filter is over-confident, because a common-mode error passes straight through a static-state Kalman filter (the gains sum to one).
